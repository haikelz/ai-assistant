# Glossary

- **Harness:** repository policy, tooling, and durable workflow state.
- **Lane:** tiny, normal, or high-risk change classification.
- **Story packet:** contract, design, execution, and validation for one outcome.
- **Durable state:** local ignored Harness SQLite database, separate from
  product finance SQLite state.
- **Domain:** finance, jobsearch, or AI/provider business boundary.
- **Provider boundary:** external AI, job-board, messaging, spreadsheet, or
  other network service.
- **Target architecture:** intended Fiber modular monolith, not current proof.
- **Trace:** structured record of actions, evidence, outcome, and friction.
